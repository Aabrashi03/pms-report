Complete the form in preparation for the annual review discussion.

Employee draft
HR master dataPosition, department and reporting-line fields will be loaded from the secured employee list after Microsoft sign-in. Employees cannot amend their assigned approver, HOD or Head of Division.
Procedure and guidance notes
  • PDR discussions occur once a year. The last KPI and previous PDR must be discussed.
  • The Job Description must be reviewed and updated if necessary.
  • Managers are responsible for effectively managing staff performance and working through PDR results with HR.
  • A copy of the completed PDR form must be sent to HR according to the stated timeline.
  • Both parties should prepare notes and work examples before the approximately 40-minute review meeting.
  • Both parties sign their agreement to the outcomes of the meeting.

Performance rating scale

0–1UnsatisfactoryThe required behaviours and skills have not been demonstrated.
2Improvement NeededJob requirements are only partially fulfilled and performance improvement is essential.
3CompetentA satisfactory and acceptable level of performance; some development may be required.
4CommendableAchievements are consistently above average and exceed most objectives.
5Exceptional PerformanceAchievements consistently exceed all objectives and job requirements.
